CASE STUDY

Omid Analyzer.

A brand for Omid Financial Group, who have run algorithmic trading and market-making in Iran’s capital market since 2015 — built to read in Persian and Latin at once.

Omid Analyzer
ROLE Graphic Designer
TEAM A marketing team, with me on brand and design.
SCOPE Logomark, bilingual wordmark, product-family marks, website
LIVE omid.ir
THE PROBLEM

It had to read as serious to a trader and as legible to someone new to markets — in two scripts, across three products.

Omid Analyzer talks to two rooms at once: people who already trade and want density, and people new to markets who need the same numbers explained. Both have to believe the same thing before they use anything — that this is a serious place to put money.

A financial brand lives on screens that are almost entirely numbers, so the identity gets a couple of seconds at the top of a page and then disappears under data. And it had to hold in Persian and Latin, under three product names, without any of them looking like a different company.

Key decisions.

1

Drew the wordmark in Persian and Latin as one design, not two.

Persian and Latin share no baseline, stroke rhythm or optical centre. I matched them on weight and height rather than letterform, giving up some of the Persian’s stroke contrast so the pair reads as one mark at the same size.

2

Gave the product family one system: OMIDMax, OMIDHub, OMIDPlus.

One construction stays fixed — the mark, the OMID lockup, the spacing — and only the suffix changes. A fourth product is a word, not a redraw.

3

Used the galaxy to say the market is a system, not noise.

Trading brands sell chaos: red and green candles, arrows, urgency. The nebula says the opposite — vast, ordered, readable from a distance. It stays dark and out of focus, so type always sits on top of it rather than in it.

OUTCOME

It launched, and the family still holds.

The brand is live on omid.ir: the galaxy world, the bilingual wordmark and the one-construction family went live with it, and Omid Financial Group has been running algorithmic trading and market-making in Iran’s capital market since 2015.
