ABOUT ME

I work across brand and interface — the identity, the illustration language, and the screens people actually use.

I am doing a master’s in Engineering of Socio-Technical Systems at the University of Oldenburg, after a computer science degree and six years designing products. Right now I am a Web & UI/UX designer at Oldenburg and OFFIS, where I rebuilt the SPP 2199 research site and serve as Web & Design Chair for Mensch und Computer 2027.
